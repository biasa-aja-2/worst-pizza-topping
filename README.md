## The line

> Try our laundry-day pizza: soggy socks, dryer lint, and one lost earring on a crust of pure regret, with a free tetanus shot on the side.

## Why this line

It names the pizza and sells a topping that is concretely, vividly awful — soggy socks, dryer lint, a lost earring — and the free tetanus shot on the side lands the joke on the last words. Specific, visual, and spoken like a real pitch.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`): a single line, 137 characters, valid UTF-8, no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
